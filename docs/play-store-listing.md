## Full description
Wake up like Mom is still calling you.

Like Home is an alarm clock where a loving but dramatic Mom wakes you up. The first ring is sweet. Snooze, and she gets annoyed. Snooze again and she starts comparing you to Sharma ji's son. Snooze a third time and the bucket of water is coming up the stairs. After that, there is no snooze button. Mom has spoken.

To stop the alarm, prove you're really out of bed:
• Walk 30 steps
• Shake your phone 20 times
• Solve three quick sums
• Photograph the spot you chose, like your bathroom sink
• Say "I'm awake, Mummy!" out loud
• Type today's promise, word for word

THE WHOLE FAMILY
Mummy, Papa (few words, very disappointed), Dadi (halwa and your childhood stories), the Boss (calendar invites at 7 am) and Abuela (café con leche, and the chancla).

NINE WAYS TO RING
Mom texts you, Comic drama, Breaking news, Retro game boss fight, Filmy drama with a sad violin, Warm kitchen, Calm night, Fridge notes, or Smart mix.

HER REAL VOICE
Record your own Mom for every drama level. Lines in English, Hinglish and Hindi, read aloud with your phone's voice when you haven't recorded one.

MORNING REPORT CARD
Snoozes, wake-up time, no-snooze streak, badges like Early Bird and Snooze King, and Mom's monthly verdict. One tap to share with friends, plus Mom's WhatsApp sticker pack.

BUILT TO ACTUALLY WAKE YOU
• Exact alarms that ring over the lock screen, even on silent
• Gradual volume, a backup tone, and a volume floor so a muted alarm still rings
• Snoozes that survive restarts and clock changes
• Step-by-step battery settings for Xiaomi, Samsung, Vivo, Oppo, Realme, OnePlus and Honor

EXTRAS
Bedtime, water and breakfast reminders in Mom's voice · weekend mode with longer snoozes · a 20-minute power nap tile · home screen widget · 12/24-hour clocks · dark, comfortable design

Your alarms, photos and recordings stay on your phone. No account. No ads.

## Permission declarations (Play Console)
- **Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM):** Like Home is an alarm clock; its core function is to ring at the exact time the user sets.
- **Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT):** shows the ringing alarm over the lock screen so the user can stop or snooze it.
- **Foreground service (media playback):** plays the alarm sound and the character's voice while the alarm rings.
- **Physical activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION):** counts steps for the optional "walk 30 steps" wake-up task.
- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** records the user's own voice clips and powers the optional "say it out loud" task.

## Data safety form
- Data collected: App activity and crash logs (only when Firebase is configured), not linked to identity, used for analytics and app stability.
- Data shared: none.
- Photos, audio and alarms: processed on device only, not collected.
- Encryption in transit: yes (Firebase). Users can request deletion: uninstalling removes all on-device data.
