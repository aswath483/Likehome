Privacy Policy

Like Home · Effective 25 October 2026

In short: your alarms, mornings, photos and recordings stay on your phone. There are no accounts and no ads.

What stays on your phone

  • Your alarms, settings, wake-up history, streaks and badges.
  • The times you went to bed (when you open the bedside clock or sleep sounds in the evening), for sleep insights. Only the last four weeks are kept.
  • Reference and morning photos for the photo check. Morning photos are compared and deleted straight away.
  • Mom's WhatsApp number, if you add it. It is only used to open WhatsApp with a message ready for you to send.
  • Voice clips you record. They are only played back on your phone when your alarm rings.

None of this is uploaded by Like Home. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Public holidays

If an alarm skips public holidays, Like Home downloads your country's holiday list from date.nager.at, a free public service. The request contains only the country and year, nothing about you.

Weather

If you turn on the weather in the morning briefing, Like Home asks open-meteo.com, a free public weather service, for today's forecast. The request contains only your approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, and nothing else about you. The answer is kept on your phone for the day.

Purchases

If you buy Like Home Premium, Google Play handles the payment; Like Home never sees your card. Like Home uses RevenueCat to check what you bought: it receives an anonymous id for the app on your phone and your Google Play purchase details (product, purchase token, dates, country and currency), under RevenueCat's own privacy policy. No name or email is sent.

Backups

If Android backup is on for your phone, it copies your alarms, settings, mornings, photos and voice clips to your own Google account, under Google's policy, so they return on a new phone. You can also save a backup file yourself from Settings; it stays wherever you put it. Like Home never sees either.

Live family mode

Only if you link with family. Then Like Home stores, in Google Firebase: an anonymous id for each linked phone (no name, email or phone number is needed), the name you type for the person being woken, the alarms your family sets, and the latest alarm event (ringing, snoozed, up or missed, with the time). Only linked family members can read it. Unlinking removes the link at once.

Streak challenge

Only if you start or join a challenge with friends. Then Like Home stores, in Google Firebase: an anonymous id for your phone, the name you choose to show, the challenge's start date and length, and for each morning of the challenge the date and how many times you snoozed. Only the players in that challenge can read it. Leaving removes you from it.

Permissions, and why

  • Alarms & reminders, notifications, full-screen alerts: to ring on time and show the alarm over the lock screen.
  • Physical activity (optional): only for the "walk 30 steps" task, only while the alarm rings.
  • Microphone (optional): only for recording a voice and for the "say it out loud" task. Speech is handled by your phone's speech recogniser, which may use your phone maker's or Google's service under their own policy.
  • Calendar (optional): only for "Earlier for early meetings" and the morning briefing. Like Home reads the start time and title of your first event of the day, on your phone, and never stores or sends it.
  • Approximate location (optional): only for Ramadan mode and the weather in the morning briefing. It is rounded to about 1 km and kept in your settings. Prayer times are worked out on your phone; for the weather, only the rounded location is sent to open-meteo.com.
  • Barcode scan (optional): only for the barcode task. Google Play services' scanner reads the code on your phone and hands Like Home just the code's number, which is kept with the alarm.
  • Camera: Like Home asks your phone's camera app to take the photo-check pictures; it has no camera permission of its own.

Usage and crash data

To find bugs and see which features people use, Like Home may use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They collect anonymous app events (for example, "alarm rang", "snoozed", "report shared"), device type and crash reports. They do not collect your name, contacts, photos or recordings. See Firebase privacy.

Sharing

When you share a morning report or add the sticker pack, the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) receives that image under its own policy. Like Home sells nothing and shares nothing else.

Children

Like Home is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
